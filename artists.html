TAN CHOON GHEE WATERCOLOUR · 1962
Sorotan Pelukis

Tan Choon Ghee

Penang's best-loved watercolourist spent a lifetime recording the island's streets, jetties, and kampung. The gallery holds two of his works — line first, colour second, and never a stroke more than the subject needs.
